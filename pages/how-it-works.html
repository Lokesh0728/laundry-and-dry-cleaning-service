Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How do I schedule a laundry pickup? +

Simply log in to your customer account, select the services you need, choose a pickup date and time slot, add your address, and confirm your order.

Can I choose a specific pickup time? +

Yes. You can select from the available pickup time slots shown during booking.

Do you provide both per-kg and per-item services? +

Yes. Everyday laundry services such as Wash & Fold can be priced by weight, while specialized services such as Dry Cleaning and Steam Press are generally priced per item.

How can I track my order? +

After your garments are collected, you can track your order through your customer dashboard as it moves through each processing stage.

What happens after my clothes are collected? +

Your garments are inspected and sorted according to their fabric and care requirements before the appropriate cleaning process begins.

Can you remove difficult stains? +

We offer stain-treatment services for selected garments. Results depend on the type of stain, fabric, and how long the stain has been present.

Do you handle delicate fabrics? +

Yes. Our Premium Fabric Care service is designed for selected delicate, luxury, and specialty fabrics. Garments may be assessed before treatment.

Can I order dry cleaning along with regular laundry? +

Yes. You can combine multiple eligible services within the same pickup order.

How long does the service take? +

Turnaround time depends on the service, garment type, order volume, and pickup location. The estimated delivery time will be provided during the booking process.

Do you offer express service? +

Express service may be available for selected services and locations, subject to availability and additional charges.

Will I receive an invoice? +

Yes. Your completed orders and billing history are available through your customer dashboard, where eligible invoices can be viewed or downloaded.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule my pickup? +

You can manage eligible upcoming orders through your customer dashboard. Cancellation and rescheduling availability may depend on the order status and applicable policy.

Do you deliver to my area? +

You can check whether your location is within our service coverage through the Coverage Area page or during the booking process.
