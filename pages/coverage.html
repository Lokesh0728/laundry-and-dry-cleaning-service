Coverage FAQs

Everything About Our Service Areas

How can I check whether you deliver to my area? +

Enter your PIN code, locality, or neighborhood in the coverage checker to see whether pickup and delivery are currently available.

Do you provide pickup and delivery in every service area? +

Available services and time slots can vary by location. Your options will be shown when you enter your address.

Can I schedule a pickup outside your listed areas? +

Orders can generally only be scheduled within active coverage zones. You can request notification for areas that are not yet served.

Do all services have the same coverage? +

Not necessarily. Specialized services such as Premium Fabric Care or Express Service may have additional location restrictions.

Can I choose my pickup time? +

Yes. Available time slots are displayed based on your location and operational availability.

Is doorstep delivery included? +

Doorstep pickup and delivery availability depends on the selected service and location. Any applicable delivery charges will be shown clearly before booking.

Are new areas being added? +

Yes. Coverage can expand as our pickup and delivery network grows.

Can businesses request regular pickup and delivery? +

Yes. Selected business customers can enquire about recurring laundry, uniform, towel, and linen-care services.
